# Training and Certification Policy

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out which roles at the firm need certification, how long each certificate stays valid, and who keeps the evidence. It applies to every employee and to contractors who touch patient records, payments or production systems.

## 2. Roles that require certification

Clinical staff must hold a current First Aid certificate.

Treasury staff must complete AML training annually.

All staff must complete security awareness training each year.

A person whose required certificate is missing or expired may not perform the tasks that depend on it until a valid certificate is on file.

## 3. Validity

A certificate is valid through the end date printed on it. The expiry date itself counts as a valid day; the certificate lapses the day after.

Certificates are only accepted when they come from the accredited issuer named on the record.

## 4. Renewal

Staff must renew each certificate at least 30 days before it expires.

Managers are responsible for chasing renewals in their team and for approving time for the training.

## 5. Evidence and records

The evidence locker holds a copy of every certificate as it was checked.

Certification expiry dates must be reported to managers each month.

Training records must be kept for three years after a person leaves the firm.

## 6. Review of this policy

The compliance team reviews this policy every January and records the review date.
