# AML Procedure

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how the firm detects, reviews and reports suspicious activity. It applies to the treasury team and to anyone who reviews customer transactions.

## 2. Patterns just under the limit

Cash deposits that stay just under the reporting limit must be reviewed and reported when they form a pattern.

Cash activity inside the just-under-limit band must be reviewed every working day.

The reporting limit used by this procedure is USD 10,000 in funds or other assets.

## 3. Escalation and reporting

Staff must escalate any attempt to split a payment to avoid a reporting threshold.

The annual independent review of this procedure must be completed each February.

## 4. Customer due diligence

Customers must be identified before an account is opened.

## 5. Confidentiality

A suspicious activity report must never be disclosed to the subject of the report.
