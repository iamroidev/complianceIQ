# Access Control Policy

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who may access which systems, how duties are separated, and how patient records are protected. It applies to every account that can reach firm data.

## 2. Separation of duties

No one person may both change production code and deploy it to production without a second person approving the deploy.

Privileged accounts must be limited to the people who need them for their role.

Access to payment systems must be limited to the treasury team.

## 3. Patient records

Restricted patient records may only be viewed by members of the care team.

Staff must ask only for the records they need for the task in front of them.

Care team lists must be updated the same day a patient changes ward.

## 4. Accounts

Every privileged account must have multi-factor authentication enabled.

Access must be removed within one business day of a person leaving the firm.

Shared logins are not permitted for any system that holds personal data.

## 5. Reviews

Access reviews must be completed every January.

The compliance team spot-checks privileged accounts every quarter.
