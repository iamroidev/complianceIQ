# Travel and Expense Policy

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what the firm pays for when people travel or spend on its behalf, and what evidence must accompany each claim.

## 2. Claiming

Expenses of USD 75 or more must be supported by a receipt.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the spend.

Mileage is paid at the standard rate and requires a logged journey.

## 3. Approval

A manager must approve every expense claim before reimbursement.

Overnight travel must be booked through the approved travel desk.

## 4. Closing the books

Expense reports for the previous month must be closed by the 10th.

Every member of staff must acknowledge the expense policy each January.
