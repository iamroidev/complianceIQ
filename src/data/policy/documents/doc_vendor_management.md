# Vendor Management Policy

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers how vendors are chosen, documented, reviewed and removed. It applies to every third party that can reach our systems or handle our data.

## 2. Documents required by tier

Critical-tier vendors must provide a current SOC 2 report, a signed data processing agreement and proof of insurance.

Standard-tier vendors must provide a signed data processing agreement.

A signed contract must be in place before any vendor starts work.

## 3. Keeping documents current

Insurance certificates must be renewed annually and the renewal evidence must be filed with the vendor record.

Data processing agreements must be renewed before they expire.

## 4. Review and renewal

Each vendor must be reviewed at least annually by its named owner, and the review date must be recorded on the vendor record.

Vendor risk ratings must be refreshed each quarter.

## 5. Offboarding

Vendor access must be revoked within 30 days of contract termination.

Any data held by the vendor must be returned or destroyed, and a certificate of destruction must be filed.

## 6. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the compliance officer and are recorded in the audit record.
