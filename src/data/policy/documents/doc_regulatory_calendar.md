# Regulatory Calendar

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This calendar lists every filing and periodic obligation the firm must meet. Each entry names the deadline, the owner and the evidence that proves it was done.

## 2. Recurring filings

A suspicious activity summary must be filed within fifteen days of each quarter end.

A suspicious activity report must be filed within 30 calendar days of initial detection of the facts that support it.

Currency transaction reports must be filed within 15 days of the reportable transaction.

## 3. Corporate filings

Quarterly tax filings are due at the end of April, July, October and January.

The annual summary of filed reports must be delivered to the board each January.

## 4. Licences and registrations

Our operating licence must be renewed every two years, and the renewal must be submitted at least 60 days before it expires.

## 5. Horizon scanning

Regulatory changes must be reviewed monthly by the compliance team.

The review notes are kept in the compliance workspace and are not tied to a single deadline.
