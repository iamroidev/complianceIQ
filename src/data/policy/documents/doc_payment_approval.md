# Payment Approval Policy

Demo policy document · version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who may approve a payment and how many approvers each size of payment needs. Every payment released from the banking portal must follow these rules.

## 2. Approval thresholds

Payments of USD 5,000 or more require one approver who did not request the payment.

Payments of USD 25,000 or more require two distinct approvers.

Wire transfers above USD 50,000 require approval by the treasury manager in addition to the two distinct approvers.

## 3. Separation

The requester may never approve their own payment.

Two approvals from the same person count as one.

## 4. Evidence

The approval evidence for each payment must be retained with the payment record.

Balances must be reconciled monthly by someone outside the payments team.

## 5. Exceptions

Emergency payments below USD 5,000 may be approved after the fact by the finance director, and the exception must be written up the same week.

Payment policy exceptions must be logged and reviewed each quarter.
